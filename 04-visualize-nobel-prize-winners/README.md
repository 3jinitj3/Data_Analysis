During this project I analyized noble prize winners data. Used data to see the proportional growth of US winners compared to the total by decades, which showed a steady increase. 
Also, I was able to see the proportion of female winners and their categories with literature and peace being the top categories for woman.
This project has allowed me to put the skills I have learned from DataCamp to practice and given me new ideas into manipulating data and visualization.
